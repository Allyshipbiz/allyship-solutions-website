Article I

How a deal moves through Allyship

Source

Off-market and on-market leads are pulled from direct outreach, agent relationships, and an open property-submission channel — not mass-marketed lists.

Underwrite

Every property is run through the same repair-adjusted offer formula before it's presented to anyone. No deal skips the math.

Structure & close

Assignment, wholesale, or JV — whichever structure fits the deal and the capital behind it, documented with a standing purchase & sale template.

Standing offer formula Max Offer = (ARV × 0.70) − Repairs
Article II

Investment criteria

Geography

Richmond, VA and the surrounding Central Virginia MSA. We know these streets — we don't underwrite outside them.

Property types

Single-family, small multifamily, and select land plays (including adaptive-use opportunities where the numbers support it).

Deal structure

Wholesale assignment, double close, or joint venture — matched to what the seller needs and what the capital partner wants.

Condition

Value-add and distressed preferred. Repair scope is estimated up front, not discovered after close.

Allyship starts as the connector between deals and capital. As lending capacity builds, these move from roadmap to live service.
